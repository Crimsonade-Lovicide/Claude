# Research: Case 03, The Mignonette

**Question:** Is killing to survive murder?
**Anchor:** South Atlantic, July 1884. The yacht *Mignonette*, sailing from England to Sydney,
sank in a gale. Four men took to a 13-foot dinghy. On the twentieth day Captain Tom Dudley, with
the mate Edwin Stephens's agreement, killed the 17-year-old cabin boy Richard Parker, and the
three survivors ate him. They told the authorities everything. *R v Dudley and Stephens* (1884)
14 QBD 273: necessity is no defence to murder. Sentenced to death; commuted to six months.
**Bigger question:** Can necessity ever justify killing an innocent person, and if it can, who
gets to decide?

Tags: `RECORD` = in a primary source (law report, special verdict, statute, court decision,
archive catalogue, the period text itself). `INFERENCE` = reliable press or secondary sources, or
our reasoning from the record. `UNCERTAIN` = sources disagree or unverified; don't state on
screen without the hedge shown. Researched 18 Oct 2026.

Before air, spot-check every on-screen quote against the page cited. We could not open BAILII
or the 14 QBD scan directly; the judgment text below is checked against two independent
transcriptions plus the passages quoted in *Re A* [2001] Fam 147. Simpson's book is not online
(archive.org copy is lending-only); his page numbers come via Wikipedia and must be checked in a
library copy before any on-screen attribution to him.

**Names.** Tom Dudley (court papers: Thomas). Edwin Stephens (the special verdict misprints
"Edward"). Edmund Brooks (some sources "Edward"). Richard Parker. Rescue ship *Moctezuma*
(some sources "Montezuma"). **We say Tom Dudley, Edwin Stephens, Edmund Brooks, Richard Parker,
the Moctezuma.**

## F. Care rules

- Richard Parker was 17 (A4). TNA's records specialist calls him "a 17-year-old orphan" and
  Dudley said he "had nobody" [TNA-POD; SJ]; tag INFERENCE. He had at least one brother, Daniel
  (C3), so say "an orphan," never "alone in the world."
- No graphic depiction. The verdict's sentence (B6) is the limit; no blood, no butchery, no
  "What me?" (a disputed later account, [SIMPSON] p.67). No reenactment with actors.
- We depict no one: not Parker, Dudley, Stephens or Brooks. Use the special verdict text, the
  1884 dinghy photograph [COPY], period engravings (credited), the memorial, and an empty
  reconstructed dinghy tagged RECONSTRUCTION.
- Brooks was never charged and opposed the killing in the record (B4); don't call him a killer.
- The memorial (D12) is a family's grave: film it respectfully or not at all.
