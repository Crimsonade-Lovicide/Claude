# Channel trailer

**Length:** about 75 s (16:9) plus a 30 s vertical cut for Shorts.
**Job:** tell a stranger in under ten seconds what the show is and why it's different
(it commits to a verdict and a confidence level), then show the range of cases.
**Assets:** every shot reuses Episode 01's builds (Scale, corridor, brain, verdict meter,
cards) or is typographic, so the trailer needs nothing new. Same narrator as the episodes.

Narration is the `>` lines. Shot IDs are prefixed T.

---

### T01 · Cold
**Picture:** `3D: scale` Black. The Scale fades up out of darkness, level, slow push.
> Some questions never get settled.

### T02 · Case 01
**Picture:** `3D: brain` Glass brain, the tumor glowing on. Caption "CASE 01 · VIRGINIA, 2000."
> A man's brain changes, and so does everything he does. Is he guilty?

### T03 · Case 02
**Picture:** `GFX: words` "TEMPE, ARIZONA · MARCH 18, 2018" typing on over black, a road's centre line scrolling under it.
> A car driving itself hits a woman crossing the road. Who's guilty, when no one was driving?

### T04 · Case 03
**Picture:** `GFX: words` "THE MIGNONETTE · 1884" over a slow, dark sea gradient.
> Four men in a lifeboat. Three come home. Is killing to survive murder?

### T05 · Case 04
**Picture:** `GFX: words` "THE PERNKOPF ATLAS" on a closed, untitled book cover rendered as type; no plates shown.
> A medical atlas drawn from the bodies of people executed by the Nazis. Should surgeons still use it?

### T06 · The method
**Picture:** `GFX: card` Three cards land one after another: RECORD, INFERENCE, ARGUMENT stamps. Then `3D: scale` cards drop on both pans.
> Most shows lay out both sides and stop there. We go through the evidence piece by piece: what's on the record, what's inference, and what's only an argument.

### T07 · The verdict
**Picture:** `GFX: verdict` The verdict meter fills to 70% with "WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND" fading up.
> And then we do the part everyone else skips. We give a verdict. We tell you how sure we are. And we tell you exactly what would change our mind.

### T08 · Open
**Picture:** `3D: scale` The scale tips, then settles, still moving slightly.
> Because the verdict stays open. New evidence, or a better argument from you, can move it.

### T09 · Title
**Picture:** `GFX: title` WEIGHT OF EVIDENCE / *One question. The evidence. A verdict.* Then "CASE 01 · THE TUMOR DEFENSE · OUT NOW."
> Weight of Evidence. Case one is out now.
